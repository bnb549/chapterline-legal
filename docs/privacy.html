Privacy Policy

Chapterline is an offline-first audiobook player for iPhone, made by Ben Monroe. This policy describes what the app stores and what it does not send anywhere.

Effective date: October 5, 2026.

What Chapterline is

Chapterline plays DRM-free audiobook files you already have. It is not a store, not a catalog, and not a streaming service. There is no Chapterline account and no Chapterline server.

Data that stays on your device

The app stores these items in its local sandbox so playback can resume:

  • Audiobook files you import, copied as-is into the app. They are not re-encoded.
  • Cover artwork extracted from those files, or artwork you set.
  • Book title, chapters, bookmarks, and playback position.
  • Settings such as skip interval, speed, appearance, and sleep fade.
  • Optional listening stats, only if you leave tracking on. Stats can be exported as a JSON file you choose to share, or deleted in Settings.

Audio files live in the app’s Documents folder. Progress and settings live in on-device storage. A shared app group is used only so the share extension and the home-screen widget on the same device can hand a file or a Now Playing snapshot to the app. None of that is uploaded by Chapterline.

What Chapterline does not collect

  • No account, name, email, or phone number.
  • No advertising identifier and no ads.
  • No analytics or tracking SDK.
  • No sale or share of personal information.
  • No library, progress, or stats sent to a Chapterline server. There isn’t one.

Import uses the Files picker or the share sheet. Playback uses Apple’s audio, Lock Screen, and optional CarPlay Now Playing interfaces. Those are Apple system features on your device. Chapterline does not require a network connection to play a book you have already imported.

If you turn on Apple’s “Share with App Developers” crash reports in iOS Settings, Apple may send diagnostic data to the developer under Apple’s own privacy policy. Chapterline does not add its own crash reporter.

Files you import

You choose what to import. Chapterline rejects Audible .aa and .aax files and does not unlock DRM. Imported files remain in the app until you delete the book or delete the app.

Deletion

Delete a book in the app to remove that book’s files and progress. Delete all listening stats in Settings to remove sessions and totals. Deleting the app removes the remaining sandbox data, including imported files and settings.

Children

Chapterline is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
