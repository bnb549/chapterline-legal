Chapterline Support

Chapterline is a local, DRM-free audiobook player for iPhone. No account is required.

Developer: Ben Monroe.

Contact

Open a public issue and include the iOS version, the Chapterline version from Settings, and what you expected to happen:

https://github.com/bnb549/Chapterline/issues

App source: https://github.com/bnb549/Chapterline

Common limits

  • Audible .aa and .aax files are not supported. Chapterline does not unlock DRM.
  • Playback does not need an account or a network connection after the file is imported.
  • Listening stats, if enabled, stay on the device. Export or delete them in Settings.
  • CarPlay Now Playing works with the system player. Browsing the library from the car needs Apple’s CarPlay Audio entitlement.
